I am an art and architectural historian working as a writer, researcher, curator and critic. My interests centre on the relationship between architecture and the visual arts from the twentieth century onwards, critical spatial practices in contemporary art, and queer themes and methodologies. My writing has appeared in De Witte Raaf, Stedelijk Studies and Metropolis M, and I am an editor at Simulacrum Magazine. Previously, I was commissioned by the Association of Dutch Art Historians to compile the longlist for the Karel van Mander Prize, which recognises an outstanding art-historical publication, and by the Cultural Heritage Agency of the Netherlands to research post-war office buildings.

I hold both a BA and an MA in Art History (2025, cum laude), both from the University of Amsterdam, and was a visiting student at TU Delft and the University of Technology Sydney. At the Stedelijk Museum Amsterdam, I worked as a curatorial intern (2024) and floormanager for the public programme (2021–2023). I contributed to the exhibitions The Don Quixote Sculpture Hall (2024), stanley brouwn (2024), Ana Lupas (2024) and Anselm Kiefer (2025).

Alongside my work in the arts, I hold a position at the University of Amsterdam, where I support researchers and students in the Humanities Faculty in strengthening the societal impact of their work.
